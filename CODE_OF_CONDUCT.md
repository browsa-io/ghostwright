# Code of conduct

Ghostwright is built in the open, and everyone who takes part in it (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) is expected to make it a place where people are glad to help.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. People here have different backgrounds, first languages and levels of experience.
- Talk about the work: the code, the idea, the evidence. Not about the person.
- Give and take review in good faith. Say what you saw and what you would change; assume the other person wants the same good result.
- Own your mistakes, and help others recover from theirs.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or attacks on who someone is.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing a discussion, or spam.

## If something goes wrong

Write to **support@browsa.io** with "Ghostwright conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the maintainers at Browsa and kept private. We will look into it, tell you what we decided, and act: a request to stop, removing a comment, or blocking an account from the project when it is needed.

Maintainers follow the same rules, and may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break them.

This applies in the project's spaces and wherever someone speaks for the project.
